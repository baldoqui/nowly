Parents Raise Concerns Over School Portraits Resembling Mug Shots

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 21:00 UTC · 1 source

Parents are voicing frustration and humor after receiving school portraits of their children that they say resemble emotionless mug shots rather than traditional keepsakes, according to a report by The Wall Street Journal.

Whitney Friedericksen, a mother residing near Houston, shared her reaction after receiving the picture day portrait of her 10-year-old son, Greyden. She noted that while her son is naturally adventurous, sweet, and funny, he refuses to smile for formal photographs, resulting in a completely expressionless image.

While Friedericksen viewed her son's portrait with amusement, other parents have expressed disappointment, emphasizing that annual school photos should capture students' milestones and genuine personalities. Despite the dissatisfaction and rising costs of student photo packages in recent years, families continue to purchase them.

The trend has sparked widespread discussion on social media and among families regarding how school photo sessions are conducted. As parents share the stark contrast between their children's everyday expressions and their formal picture day portraits, many are advocating for photographers and schools to adopt more relaxed and flexible approaches.
