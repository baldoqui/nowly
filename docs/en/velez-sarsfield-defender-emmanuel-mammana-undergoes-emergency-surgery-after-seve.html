Vélez Sarsfield Defender Emmanuel Mammana Undergoes Emergency Surgery After Severe Chest Trauma

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Vélez Sarsfield defender Emmanuel Mammana was hospitalized in intensive care in Rosario on September 12, 2026, after undergoing emergency surgery for multiple rib fractures and a pneumothorax sustained during a match against Newell's Old Boys.

The incident occurred ten minutes into the second half of the 1-1 draw when Vélez goalkeeper Tomás Marchiori accidentally collided knee-first into Mammana's chest while jumping to clear a cross. Mammana was immediately replaced by Lisandro Magallán and rushed to the Sanatorio de la Mujer in Rosario. According to Vélez Sarsfield, medical evaluations confirmed right hemithorax trauma with five fractured ribs—one of which was displaced and punctured his lung—and a pneumothorax. Surgeons placed a pleural drainage tube to evacuate trapped air and stabilize his breathing. Club president Fabián Berlanga confirmed that Mammana remains stable in intensive care as doctors assess whether a secondary surgery is required to fix the costal fractures before authorizing his transfer to Buenos Aires.

A pneumothorax occurs when air leaks into the space between the lung and chest wall, leading to partial or complete collapse of the lung. Mammana, a former River Plate defender and founder of Mammana Fútbol Club, is expected to be sidelined for two to three months while he recovers.
