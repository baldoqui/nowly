Argentine Naval Prefecture Seizes Over 57 Million Pesos in Smuggled Goods Along Uruguay River

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

Members of the Argentine Naval Prefecture intercepted a major smuggling operation along the Uruguay River near Concepción del Uruguay, seizing contraband valued at more than 57 million Argentine pesos after suspects abandoned a boat and a vehicle.

The operation commenced during the early morning hours at kilometer 205 of the Uruguay River when officers on patrol spotted an unregistered boat carrying two individuals alongside a Uruguayan-registered utility van parked on a nearby dirt road. When officers issued a verbal order to halt, the suspects fled into the surrounding terrain, according to EL ARGENTINO. Following a search of the secured perimeter, authorities inspected the abandoned vehicle and discovered 16 packages containing apparel, footwear, and veterinary products intended for illegal export to Uruguay.

The case was submitted to the Federal Court of Concepción del Uruguay, presided over by Substitute Federal Judge Hernán Sergio Viri, who ordered the seizure of all cargo, the utility vehicle, and the vessel. The coordinated operation involved personnel from the Prefectures of Concepción del Uruguay and Colón, as well as the Criminal Intelligence and Investigations Delegation of the Lower Uruguay Zone Prefecture.
