Macy's to Honor Beloved Cincinnati Zoo Hippo Fiona in 100th Thanksgiving Parade

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 19:15 UTC · 3 sources

Retailer Macy's has announced that it will honor Fiona, the beloved Cincinnati Zoo hippopotamus who recently passed away, during the upcoming 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

According to company statements and local news reports, the tribute will mark the return of Macy's Blue Hippo balloon, which originally debuted in the parade in 1931. Balloon handlers will wear purple patches in memory of Fiona during the event. Macy's also confirmed it has made a donation to the Cincinnati Zoo with the institution's blessing. The announcement follows social media petitions from fans seeking a tribute for the animal following her death on Nov. 19. Veterinary reports indicated the 9-year-old hippo passed away after ingesting a hat that could not be surgically removed after becoming stuck in her small intestine.

Fiona gained international fame when she was born six weeks premature in 2017, becoming the smallest hippopotamus on record to survive infancy and capturing the hearts of animal lovers worldwide.
