Argentine Singers La Joaqui and Tiago PZK Confirm Relationship Following Family Gathering

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Argentine urban music artists La Joaqui and Tiago PZK have publicly signaled their romantic relationship during a family gathering in Florencio Varela, putting an end to weeks of media speculation following La Joaqui's breakup with singer Luck Ra.

The news surfaced after Vanesa Aranda, mother of the late musician El Noba, published photographs of a family lunch introducing Tiago PZK as her new son-in-law on Instagram. The images showed the two artists embracing and smiling during the gathering. According to reporting from journalist Yanina Latorre, the bond between the musicians, who have known each other since adolescence, strengthened when Tiago visited La Joaqui during a unpublicized hospital stay two weeks prior. The public confirmation caused tension with La Joaqui's former partner Luck Ra, who voiced hurt over her dating an associate he previously considered a close friend. In response, La Joaqui issued a statement criticizing her ex-partner for publicly airing private matters while asserting her independent history with Tiago.

La Joaqui shared a close, sibling-like relationship with El Noba, whose real name was Lautaro Coronel, before his death in a motorcycle accident in June 2022. The pair collaborated on songs such as Lassie, Pica, and Butakera, and La Joaqui has remained closely connected to Coronel's family since his passing.
