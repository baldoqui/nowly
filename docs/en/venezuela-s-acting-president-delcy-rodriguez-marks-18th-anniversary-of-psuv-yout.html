Venezuela's Acting President Delcy Rodríguez Marks 18th Anniversary of PSUV Youth Movement

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

CARACAS — Venezuelan Acting President Delcy Rodríguez issued a congratulatory message on September 12, 2026, to celebrate the 18th anniversary of the founding of the Youth of the United Socialist Party of Venezuela (JPSUV), highlighting the role of young generations in the nation's political and social development.

In a message published via her official Telegram channel, Rodríguez praised the youth organization for demonstrating nearly two decades of consistent growth and active participation across the country. She called on the young political cadre to continue advancing in their daily efforts to drive national sovereignty and economic development. According to teleSUR, the anniversary message also stressed the commitment of the youth organization to supporting local communities and upholding the political legacy of former President Hugo Chávez.

According to Article 2 of the JPSUV internal statutes, the organization serves to train, organize, and integrate Venezuelan youth into political participation while working to eliminate social exclusion and discrimination. Internationally, JPSUV is a member of the World Federation of Democratic Youth and forms part of the Youth of the Great Patriotic Pole Simón Bolívar.
