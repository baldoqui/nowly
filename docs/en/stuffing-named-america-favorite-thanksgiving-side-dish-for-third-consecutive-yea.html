Stuffing Named America Favorite Thanksgiving Side Dish for Third Consecutive Year

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 02:00 UTC · 1 source

Stuffing has once again been crowned America's favorite Thanksgiving side dish, securing the top spot in 39 states for the third consecutive year, according to a survey of 5,000 Americans conducted by Campbell's.

Following stuffing, the most popular choices nationwide included mashed potatoes, sweet potatoes, macaroni and cheese, and green bean casserole. Louisiana stood out as the only state selecting yams as its top preference, while Utah recorded a tie among its favored options, according to reporting by WTVR. The survey also highlighted a major shift in holiday dining preferences, showing that 80 percent of Americans prefer side dishes over the main course, with nearly half indicating they would be satisfied with a Thanksgiving meal made up entirely of sides. Among Generation Z respondents, nearly two-thirds expressed a preference for an all-side-dish meal, as reported by NBC Philadelphia.

While traditional sides remain deeply popular—with 88 percent of respondents stating they genuinely enjoy classic dishes—consumers are also showing a willingness to innovate. Three in four respondents reported plans to add a new dish to their holiday menu this year, identifying side dishes as the preferred area for trying out new recipes.
