Death Toll Rises to 76 in Philippine Ferry Fire Near Coron

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

The death toll from a fire aboard the ferry MV June Aster off Busuanga Island near Coron in the western Philippines has risen to 76 after authorities recovered 41 additional bodies from the burned vessel.

The vessel was carrying 134 people, including 117 passengers and 17 crew members, and was traveling toward Coron in Palawan province when the fire broke out approximately 1.2 nautical miles northeast of the Marcilla neighborhood. Philippine Coast Guard spokesperson Noemie Cayabyab, cited by ABS-CBN, confirmed that the recovered remains were transferred to the port of Coron. Rescue operations have saved 43 people so far, including Chilean citizens Tábata Jara and Lucas Jofré, who survived by swimming away from the flames. Firefighters entered the ferry on Friday after extinguishing the blaze more than a day after it started, allowing access to areas previously unreachable due to extreme heat and thick smoke.

The Philippine Coast Guard and the Philippine National Police are currently working to identify the victims, while maritime authorities investigate the cause of the disaster aboard the ship owned by Atienza Inter-Island Ferries. According to a statement from the Maritime Industry Authority citing survivor testimonies, two explosions were heard inside the vessel shortly before the fire started.
