United States Defeats Spain 76-66 to Advance to World Cup Final

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

The United States women's basketball team defeated Spain 76-66 in the World Cup semifinals on Saturday, securing a spot in the championship game against France while sending Spain to the bronze medal match against Germany.

Spain opened the game with a strong 7-1 lead before the United States rallied behind Aliyah Boston and Paige Bueckers to take a 21-18 edge at the end of the first quarter. Spain stayed competitive through the middle quarters, entering the final period tied at 58-58 thanks to 17 points from guard Iyana Martin. However, the American squad pulled away in the fourth quarter by holding Spain to just eight points in the final ten minutes. Breanna Stewart led the United States with 16 points and six rebounds, while the U.S. attempted 35 free throws compared to 11 for Spain, according to EFE.

Spain will compete against Germany for the bronze medal on Sunday at 16:30, seeking a podium finish after having previously defeated Germany by 30 points in the group stage.
