Spanish National Lotteries Announce Winning Numbers for Sept. 12 Drawings

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Spain conducted its scheduled national lottery drawings on Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, revealing winning results across La Primitiva, Bonoloto, and ONCE games.

The winning combination drawn for La Primitiva was 03, 04, 22, 26, 40, and 44, with complementary number 47 and refund number 8, while Joker code 4556130 awarded a 1-million-euro prize. No tickets matched all numbers for the main jackpot in La Primitiva, raising the accumulated prize pool to an estimated 17.5 million euros for the next drawing. Meanwhile, the Bonoloto draw yielded winning numbers 03, 04, 12, 23, 37, and 48, with complementary number 07 and refund number 8. According to La Nueva España, four second-category Bonoloto tickets successfully matched five numbers plus the complementary number, validated across administrations in Santoña, Valladolid, Madrid, and Orihuela.

Loterías y Apuestas del Estado holds La Primitiva drawings twice weekly on Thursdays and Saturdays, while Bonoloto drawings take place daily. The ONCE organization concurrently runs weekend draws, featuring recurring monthly awards alongside lump-sum prize payouts.
