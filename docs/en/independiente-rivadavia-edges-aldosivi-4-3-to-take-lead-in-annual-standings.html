Independiente Rivadavia Edges Aldosivi 4-3 to Take Lead in Annual Standings

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Independiente Rivadavia secured a thrilling 4-3 victory over Aldosivi at the Bautista Gargantini Stadium in Mendoza on September 12 during the ninth round of Argentina's Torneo Clausura, elevating the team to the top of the annual table.

Gonzalo Rios opened the scoring for the hosts in the ninth minute after receiving a cross from Alex Arce, though Aldosivi responded quickly three minutes later when Tomas Fernandez finished past the goalkeeper following a pass from Nicolas Gaitan. Independiente Rivadavia regained the lead in the 18th minute as Jose Florentin scored with a header, a decision confirmed after a Video Assistant Referee review by referee Dario Herrera. Deep into first-half stoppage time, another VAR review resulted in a penalty for a handball by Nicolas Cordero, which Arce successfully converted to make it 3-1. According to Noticias Argentinas, Lucas Castro cut the deficit for Aldosivi with a 58th-minute header, but Arce extended the home side's advantage again in the 75th minute with his second goal of the match. Joaquin Pombo scored Aldosivi's third goal in stoppage time, but the visitors were unable to complete the comeback before the final whistle.

The win elevates Independiente Rivadavia to 48 points in the overall annual table, placing them in first position ahead of Argentinos Juniors and Velez Sarsfield. Aldosivi remains in a difficult situation near the bottom of both the annual classification and the relegation average standings with 13 points.
