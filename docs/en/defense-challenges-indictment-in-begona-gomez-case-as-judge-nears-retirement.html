Defense Challenges Indictment in Begoña Gómez Case as Judge Nears Retirement

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

MADRID — Defense lawyers for Begoña Gómez petitioned for the dismissal of judicial proceedings against her during a preliminary hearing in Madrid, contending that the indictment brief filed by popular prosecution group Hazte Oír violates Spain's Jury Trial Law.

During the court session on September 8, the Madrid Prosecutor's Office advocated for archiving the case due to a lack of evidence of criminal offenses, whereas Hazte Oír ratified its provisional charges alleging embezzlement and influence peddling. Defense attorney Jaime Campaner argued that the prosecution's filing is procedurally void because it improperly incorporates higher court rulings, Guardia Civil UCO police reports, and witness testimonies gathered during the instruction phase, which are excluded from jury proceedings. Campaner noted that the prosecution missed the statutory window under Article 31.3 of the Jury Trial Law to amend its filing prior to the conclusion of the preliminary hearing, according to reporting by Original Article.

The judicial inquiry into Gómez began in April 2024. Investigating Judge Juan Carlos Peinado, who went on leave through September 18 ahead of his mandatory retirement on September 27 at age 72, must now determine whether to archive the case, refer procedural objections to the Madrid Provincial Court, or grant the prosecution time to rectify its filing.
