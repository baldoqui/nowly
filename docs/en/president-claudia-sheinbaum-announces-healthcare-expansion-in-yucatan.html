President Claudia Sheinbaum Announces Healthcare Expansion in Yucatán

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

MÉRIDA, Mexico — Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum announced major healthcare initiatives in Yucatán on September 12, 2026, including the creation of 244 medical offices with pharmacies and the hiring of 600 healthcare professionals for the Dr. Agustín O'Horán General Hospital.

Speaking during a visit to Mérida to present the results of her second year in office, Sheinbaum stated that a primary objective of her administration is strengthening primary care across the country. According to a report by Quadratín Yucatán, the plan aims to ensure patients do not need to rely directly on hospitals or private clinics for basic consultations and medication. Sheinbaum emphasized that addressing first-contact healthcare remains a crucial priority alongside broader national health system improvements.

The measures form part of broader federal strategies in Mexico designed to improve public health services by expanding local primary care access and bolstering medical staffing.
