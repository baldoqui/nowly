Mbappe Scores Brace as Real Madrid Defeats Rayo Vallecano 4-1

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Real Madrid returned to winning ways in LaLiga with a decisive 4-1 home victory over Rayo Vallecano at the Santiago Bernabeu on Saturday, led by a two-goal performance from Kylian Mbappe.

The hosts gained early momentum in the 14th minute when Kylian Mbappe converted a penalty awarded after Rayo Vallecano defender Florian Lejeune fouled Alvaro Carreras in the penalty area. Three minutes later, Carreras doubled the advantage by finishing a pass from Mbappe. Jude Bellingham made it 3-0 in the 35th minute after connecting with a cross from Vinicius Junior. Rayo Vallecano reduced the deficit early in the second half when Sergio Camello capitalized on a defensive error to score in the 51st minute. Despite sustained pressure from the visiting side that required several saves from goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois, Mbappe sealed the victory in the 91st minute by finishing a counterattack launched by Arda Guler, as detailed by Match Report.

The win moves Real Madrid to 12 points through five matches in the 2026-27 LaLiga season as the club bounces back from its previous defeat to Betis. Manager Jose Mourinho's side next travels to face Elche on Tuesday before hosting Atletico Madrid on September 20.
