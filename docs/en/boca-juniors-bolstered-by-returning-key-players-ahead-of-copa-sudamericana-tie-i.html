Boca Juniors Bolstered by Returning Key Players Ahead of Copa Sudamericana Tie in Brazil

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Boca Juniors manager Rodolfo Arruabarrena has announced that the entire squad will travel to Brazil for the decisive second leg of their Copa Sudamericana tie against São Paulo, boosted by the return of midfielder Milton Delgado and goalkeeper Álvaro Montero.

Delgado returned to training after three weeks out with a muscle injury, while Montero rejoined the team following a trip to Colombia after the death of his grandmother. Manager Arruabarrena confirmed that the full delegation will travel to San Pablo to defend a 1-0 aggregate advantage secured by a first-leg goal from Miguel Merentiel, according to Prensa Boca. Defender Nicolás Figal also defended the squad's depth after a heavily rotated lineup secured a 3-1 domestic victory against Central Córdoba, addressing prior criticism regarding the team's alternative options.

Boca Juniors requires at least a draw at the Morumbí stadium to advance to the Copa Sudamericana semifinals. Alongside Delgado and Montero, defender Malcom Braida has recovered from a muscle strain to join the squad, whereas Ayrton Costa remains sidelined due to a hamstring injury and pubalgia.
