Lando Norris Claims Pole Position for Inaugural Madrid Grand Prix as Franco Colapinto Qualifies Ninth

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

McLaren driver Lando Norris secured pole position for the inaugural Madrid Grand Prix on Saturday, edging out Mercedes rookie Kimi Antonelli with a lap time of 1:31.824 at the newly introduced Madring circuit in Spain.

Norris snatched the top spot in the final moments of Q3, beating championship leader Antonelli by just 0.011 seconds under high temperatures in the Spanish capital. Red Bull's Max Verstappen qualified third, while Ferrari drivers Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc took fourth and fifth places respectively. Alpine's Franco Colapinto delivered an impressive performance by qualifying ninth with a time of 1:32.903, outpacing his teammate Pierre Gasly who was eliminated in Q2. As reported by LA NACION, Colapinto secured his spot in Q3 after dramatically catching a high-speed slide at 260 km/h in the heavily banked La Monumental turn. Meanwhile, local favorites experienced disappointing sessions, as both Fernando Alonso and Carlos Sainz were eliminated in Q1.

The race marks the debut of the semi-urban Madring circuit in Formula 1, featuring tight walls, high-speed sections, and a 13-degree banked turn. Grid penalties impacted the starting line-up following qualifying, including a three-place drop for Sainz for impeding Valtteri Bottas and a 40-place engine penalty for Aston Martin's Lance Stroll.
