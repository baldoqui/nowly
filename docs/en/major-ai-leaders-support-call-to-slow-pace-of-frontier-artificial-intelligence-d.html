Major AI Leaders Support Call to Slow Pace of Frontier Artificial Intelligence Development

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Executives from leading artificial intelligence firms, including Anthropic Chief Executive Dario Amodei, OpenAI Chief Executive Sam Altman, and xAI founder Elon Musk, have publicly converged on the need to moderate the pace of frontier AI development to allow safety measures to keep up with technological advances.

The industry consensus follows the publication of an essay titled "We Must Pace the Frontier" by Amodei, who warned that uncontrolled progress risks outpacing human supervision and creating severe cybersecurity hazards due to recursive self-improvement. Amodei outlined a framework starting with granting third-party evaluators permanent, employee-like access to internal systems to audit alignment, evaluate training methodologies, and report incidents. Altman publicly backed Amodei's call, confirming that OpenAI would implement equivalent independent auditing access, while Musk similarly expressed support. According to NBC News, Amodei stated that slowing the pace of frontier models could grant researchers one to two critical years to master system alignment and safety protocols before models acquire greater autonomy.

The joint calls for restraint follow heightened warnings from security researchers about intense commercial competition in the AI sector. Former Anthropic and OpenAI researcher Jacob Coxon recently resigned from Anthropic, publicly criticizing AI developers for prioritizing competitive speed over safety controls in the push toward autonomous superintelligence.
