Kim Jong Un Orders Expansion of North Korean Nuclear Arsenal After Rebuffing U.S. Outreach

Thursday, November 26, 2026, 18:30 UTC · 5 sources

SEOUL — North Korean leader Kim Jong Un inspected key facilities producing nuclear ingredients and called for an accelerated expansion of the country's nuclear arsenal, state media reported on Thursday. The public showcase came shortly after Pyongyang ignored an offer from U.S. President Donald Trump to meet during his recent trip to Asia.

According to the official Korean Central News Agency, Kim inspected two nuclear materials production factories alongside a nuclear-powered equipment base and a research facility. State media photographs showed Kim walking past rows of centrifuges designed to produce weapons-grade uranium and touring display halls containing warheads. Kim stated that bolstering the quality and quantity of North Korea's nuclear arsenal is an irreversible reality, citing perceived threats from the United States and South Korea. He specifically urged the urgent deployment of low-yield tactical nuclear weapons across multiple domains. Analysts noted that Kim's inspections are designed to demonstrate the regime's growing capabilities and reaffirm Pyongyang's refusal to engage in future denuclearization talks. South Korea's Defense Ministry responded by confirming it takes North Korea's tactical nuclear threats seriously and maintains military readiness under its alliance with Washington. Meanwhile, President Trump told reporters he remains open to reviving diplomatic communications with Kim at an appropriate time.

North Korea manufactures nuclear weapons through highly enriched uranium and plutonium programs, centered primarily at its Yongbyon complex. International Atomic Energy Agency Director General Rafael Grossi recently cautioned that North Korea's continued expansion of its enrichment infrastructure remains a subject of serious international concern.
