Chubut Governor Announces Relocation Plan for Families Affected by Cerro Hermitte Landslide

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Chubut Governor Ignacio Torres announced an immediate land allocation decree and housing relocation plan during a tour of land ceded by oil company YPF in Comodoro Rivadavia for families affected by the Cerro Hermitte ground displacement.

The initiative targets displaced residents from the Los Tilos and Marquesado neighborhoods. Governor Torres confirmed that the provincial government will prioritize surveying the land and installing basic utilities before commencing a second phase to construct new houses. According to Chubut Government Press, provincial ministers and local officials accompanied the governor during the site visit to meet directly with affected families. Torres stated that the Provincial Housing and Urban Development Institute will formalize the process to ensure comprehensive habitability solutions rather than distributing unserviced plots.

The Cerro Hermitte landslide has posed long-standing safety risks to local housing developments in Comodoro Rivadavia. Governor Torres noted that technical studies dating back to 2002 had identified the impacted areas as uninhabitable, pledging that the current provincial government will deliver fully serviced housing to resolve the long-standing residential crisis.
