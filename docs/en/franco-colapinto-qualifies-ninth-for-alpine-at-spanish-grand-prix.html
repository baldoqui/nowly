Franco Colapinto Qualifies Ninth for Alpine at Spanish Grand Prix

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

MADRID — Alpine driver Franco Colapinto secured ninth place on the starting grid for the Spanish Grand Prix on Saturday at the new Madring circuit, delivering one of his strongest qualifying performances in Formula 1.

The 23-year-old Argentine recorded a lap time of 1:32.903 in the Q3 session to finish as the fastest driver outside the top four teams, finishing ahead of Racing Bulls driver Arvid Lindblad. Colapinto advanced to the final session after placing eighth in Q2 with a time of 1:33.038, outperforming his teammate Pierre Gasly by 0.715 seconds after the Frenchman was eliminated in 14th place. According to EL ARGENTINO, Alpine executive advisor Flavio Briatore described Colapinto's drive as exceptional, while team director Steve Nielsen affirmed that ninth position represented the maximum potential result for the A526 car. Colapinto overcame a mid-session moment at Turn 12 during Q2 and made manual steering adjustments in Q3 to secure his fifth Q3 appearance since joining Alpine.

McLaren's Lando Norris claimed pole position with a lap time of 1:31.824, edging out Mercedes driver Andrea Kimi Antonelli by 0.011 seconds and Red Bull's Max Verstappen. The 57-lap Spanish Grand Prix will take place on Sunday, with Colapinto starting from the fifth row as he seeks to secure championship points.
