At Least 16 Dead in Fire at Unlicensed Chilean Senior Care Home

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

At least 16 elderly residents died after a fire swept through an unauthorized care home on Friday night in the municipality of Pitrufquén, located in Chile's southern La Araucanía region.

Ten residents survived the blaze at the single-story facility operating under the name El Edén, with survivors transferred to local medical facilities. Regional health authorities confirmed that the home had been operating without required authorization since at least 2019 and had previously received health sanctions and fines. Chilean President José Antonio Kast announced urgent nationwide inspections of elderly care facilities and ordered steps to strengthen safety regulations, while prosecutor Jorge Granada opened an investigation into the cause of the fire and summoned the facility's owners to testify, according to Europa Press.

Ministry of Social Development and Family Minister María Jesús Wulf confirmed that surviving residents will be re-housed in properly authorized Long-Term Care Facilities for Older Adults, while local municipal authorities continue providing emergency support to the victims' families.
