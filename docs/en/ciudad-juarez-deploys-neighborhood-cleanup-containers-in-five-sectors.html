Ciudad Juárez Deploys Neighborhood Cleanup Containers in Five Sectors

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

The Sanitation Department of Ciudad Juárez installed 30-cubic-meter waste containers across five neighborhoods over the weekend as part of the 'Punto Limpio a tu Colonia' initiative to help residents discard large household items and prevent illegal dumping.

Gibran Solís Kanahan, head of the Sanitation Department, stated that the initiative aims to give families a convenient way to dispose of old furniture and household clutter rather than leaving them in streets, parks, or vacant lots. According to a statement released by Juárez Municipal Government, containers were set up in the neighborhoods of Senderos de San Isidro, Urbivilla del Cedro Stage VII, Fraccionamiento Fundadores, Fraccionamiento Arecas I, and Unidad Habitacional Emiliano Zapata. Residents are encouraged to bring disused belongings directly to these collection spots.

Municipal authorities clarified that while the containers accept general household junk and small quantities of personal tires, strict restrictions remain in place. Hazardous waste, soil, construction debris, and tires sourced from commercial repair shops are prohibited from being dumped in the containers.
