Major U.S. Retailers Set Schedules for Thanksgiving and Black Friday Shopping

Thursday, November 26, 2026, 18:00 UTC · 5 sources

Major U.S. retailers have detailed their operational schedules for Thanksgiving Day and Black Friday, with nearly 187 million people expected to shop over the holiday period, according to projections from the National Retail Federation.

While primary big-box chains including Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and Costco will close their doors completely on Thanksgiving Day, select grocery chains, pharmacies, and travel stops will remain operational for last-minute shoppers. Grocery outlets such as H-E-B, Food Lion, Sprouts Farmers Market, and Rouses Markets plan to close by 2:00 p.m. or 3:00 p.m. EST, while others such as Kroger, Safeway, and Wegmans will maintain reduced schedules closing around 4:00 p.m. or 5:00 p.m. EST. Pharmacy operator Walgreens plans to keep all of its nearly 8,000 locations open during regular hours, including over 4,000 pharmacies, while most CVS locations will operate under reduced hours.

Following the holiday feast, retailers will launch early morning Black Friday operations across the country. Department store chains JCPenney and Kohl's will open at 5:00 a.m. local time, while Macy's, Target, The Home Depot, Ulta, and Bath & Body Works will open at 6:00 a.m. local time. In-store Black Friday promotions at Walmart locations will also begin at 6:00 a.m. local time.

A comprehensive guide compiled by shopping platform RetailMeNot noted that convenience chains like 7-Eleven, Circle K, Sheetz, and Wawa plan to remain open 24 hours. Conversely, outdoor retail chain REI will maintain its annual tradition of closing all locations on both Thanksgiving and Black Friday as part of its ongoing initiative.
