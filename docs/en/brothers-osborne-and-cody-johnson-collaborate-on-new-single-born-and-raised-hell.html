Brothers Osborne and Cody Johnson Collaborate on New Single 'Born and Raised Hell'

Saturday, November 21, 2026, 12:00 UTC · 5 sources

Country duo Brothers Osborne and country singer Cody Johnson have joined forces for 'Born and Raised Hell', a country-rock collaboration inspired by childhood memories and small-town adventures.

The song was released to country radio on October 22, 2026, and is featured on Brothers Osborne's album Watertown, which was released on October 23. Written by John Osborne, Kendell Marvel, and Lee Thomas Miller, the track reflects the upbringing of T.J. and John Osborne in Deale, Maryland, near the Chesapeake Bay. The lyrics draw from their youth around Rockhold Creek, exploring themes of early rebellion and outdoor adventures. Johnson's involvement stemmed from a mutual arrangement after he invited Brothers Osborne to feature on his track 'Fool Proof', prompting Johnson to contribute vocals to 'Born and Raised Hell'. The recording combines Johnson's vocal style with Brothers Osborne's country-rock sound, featuring an extended guitar solo performed by John Osborne.

Following its radio release, 'Born and Raised Hell' debuted at No. 59 on the Country Airplay chart for the week of November 21, 2026.
