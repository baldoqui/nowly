United States Defeats Spain 76-66 to Advance to Women's Basketball World Cup Final

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

BERLIN -- The United States women's basketball team secured a 76-66 victory over Spain in the World Cup semi-finals at the Berlin Arena, advancing to the gold medal match against France while pushing Spain into the bronze medal game against host nation Germany.

Spain opened the contest with strong defensive intensity, taking an early 11-4 lead before the U.S. responded to take a 21-18 edge at the end of the first quarter. The American squad capitalized on early foul calls, attempting 27 free throws in the first half compared to four for Spain to hold a 39-36 lead at halftime. Spain mounted a rally in the third quarter to tie the score at 58-58, led by 17 points each from Iyana Martin and Maria Conde. However, accumulation of fouls on key Spanish guards and an 8-0 fourth-quarter run by the Americans broke the game open, as Spain was restricted to just eight points in the final period. Breanna Stewart led the United States with 16 points and six rebounds, while Awa Fam added nine points for Spain. As reported by El Pais, Spanish head coach Miguel Mendez noted after the game that early errors in the fourth quarter proved costly against an opponent with world-class depth.

The United States enters Sunday's final seeking its fifth consecutive World Cup gold medal and aiming to extend its undefeated streak in official international competition. Spain, which previously captured World Cup silver in 2014 and bronze in 2010 and 2018, will compete for its fourth tournament medal against Germany.
