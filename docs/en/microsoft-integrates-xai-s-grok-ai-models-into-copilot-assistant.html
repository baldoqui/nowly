Microsoft Integrates xAI's Grok AI Models into Copilot Assistant

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Microsoft announced on Saturday, September 12, 2026, the integration of xAI's Grok artificial intelligence models into its Copilot assistant, initiating a preview deployment across major productivity applications including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

The deployment of the AI models developed by Elon Musk's xAI will initially run as a pilot program within key office software. However, Microsoft stated that Grok will not be accessible to Frontier clients in the European Union, the European Free Trade Association, or the United Kingdom during this preview phase. Microsoft Chief Executive Officer Satya Nadella welcomed the addition publicly on social media platform X, while Musk celebrated the release by sharing the announcement. According to reporting by EFE, the integration proceeds despite significant scrutiny regarding the tool's image generation capabilities.

The adoption of Grok into Microsoft's software suite occurs during a period of legal challenges for xAI. The firm faces a lawsuit in California alleging it commercialized features enabling non-consensual digital nudity that impacted women and minors, accusations of which Musk has denied having knowledge.
