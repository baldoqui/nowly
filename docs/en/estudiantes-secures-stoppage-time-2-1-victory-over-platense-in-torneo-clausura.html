Estudiantes Secures Stoppage-Time 2-1 Victory Over Platense in Torneo Clausura

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

Estudiantes de La Plata defeated Platense 2-1 on September 12 in the ninth round of Group A in the Torneo Clausura, securing a late victory with a 92nd-minute goal from substitute Guido Carrillo.

Both teams fielded alternative lineups in La Plata to rest starters for their upcoming Copa Libertadores second-leg matches. Adolfo Gaich opened the scoring for Estudiantes in the 19th minute by tapping in a cross from Edwin Cetre, a goal upheld following a VAR review for potential offside. Platense responded in the 24th minute when Gaston Togni crossed from the left, allowing Guido Mainero to convert at the far post past goalkeeper Fabrizio Iacovich. Estudiantes head coach Alexander Medina brought on key starters in the second half, including Joaquin Correa, Tobio Burgos, and Guido Carrillo. In the 47th minute of the second half, Carrillo capitalized on a loose ball following a cross to Eric Meza, driving a low shot into the net to bring Estudiantes to 10 points in Group A, according to Noticias Argentinas.

With the win, Estudiantes moved closer to playoff positions, sitting behind Independiente's 13 points, while Platense remains on nine points. Estudiantes will next travel to Brazil to face Corinthians on Wednesday, September 16, following a 1-1 tie in their Copa Libertadores first-leg match. Meanwhile, Platense manager Martin Palermo prepares his squad to host Fluminense in Vicente Lopez on Tuesday, aiming to overturn a 2-0 first-leg deficit.
