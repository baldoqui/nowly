Macy's to Honor Late Fiona the Hippo at 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 04:45 UTC · 5 sources

Macy's announced it will honor Fiona, the famous Cincinnati Zoo hippo who recently died, during its 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade by outfitting handlers of the returning Blue Hippo balloon with commemorative purple patches.

According to statements issued by Macy's and confirmed by local media outlets, the company secured the Cincinnati Zoo's blessing to make a financial donation in Fiona's memory and to equip the Blue Hippo balloon handlers with purple Fiona patches during the New York City parade. Fiona passed away after ingesting a knit hat that fell into her enclosure. Macy's stated on social media that Fiona brought joy to millions, prompting the company to integrate her memory into the appearance of its Blue Hippo balloon.

The Blue Hippo balloon originally debuted in the 1931 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, an era when parade balloons were released into the sky at the end of the event. The original Blue Hippo was last spotted soaring past the Empire State Building and became the only balloon from that year's parade never to be recovered. Macy's selected the historic character to return to the parade lineup as part of its 100th anniversary celebration.
