Dominican Police Arrest Three Colombian Nationals in Drugging and Robbery Scheme

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Dominican Republic authorities have arrested two Colombian women and a Colombian man in Santo Domingo Este for allegedly operating a robbery network known as 'Las Dormilonas' that seduced men, drugged their drinks, and stole their personal belongings.

According to a statement reported by EFE, agents from the Central Directorate of Investigation executed a judicial search warrant at an apartment in the Arrecifes del Sol residential complex, arresting Natali Valeria Castillo González, Laura Valentina Villegas Villafaña, and Diego Armando González. Investigators stated that the suspects established contact with victims, gained their trust, and spiked their beverages with chemical sedatives before stealing cash, jewelry, bank cards, cellular phones, and other items of value. During the raid, police recovered 197,000 Dominican pesos, Canadian dollars, Colombian pesos, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, gold jewelry, and brand-name goods linked to prior reports. Officers also confiscated a chemical substance for forensic analysis and seized a suspected counterfeit identification document held by Diego Armando González.

The joint operation was coordinated with the Public Prosecutor's Office, and the investigation remains open to identify additional victims and potential accomplices. The three detainees and seized evidence have been transferred to legal authorities for formal proceedings.
