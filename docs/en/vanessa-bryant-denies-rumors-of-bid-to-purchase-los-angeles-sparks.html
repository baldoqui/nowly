Vanessa Bryant Denies Rumors of Bid to Purchase Los Angeles Sparks

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 21:00 UTC · 1 source

Vanessa Bryant, widow of NBA legend Kobe Bryant, has publicly denied reports alleging that she is involved in a bid alongside former Los Angeles Lakers star Pau Gasol to purchase the WNBA's Los Angeles Sparks.

Addressing the claims on social media, Bryant stated that she has never engaged in discussions regarding ownership of an NBA or WNBA team and dismissed rumors initially reported by Sports Business Journal as completely false.

While Bryant refuted the reports, Gasol has not publicly commented on the speculation. A spokesperson for Gasol's firm, Gasol16 Ventures, noted that the company is open to exploring investment opportunities. Gasol, a six-time NBA All-Star and two-time champion, has previously invested in the WNBA and publicly supported women's basketball.

The ownership speculation arises as current Sparks owner Mark Walter, who purchased the franchise with Magic Johnson in 2014, reportedly seeks to sell the team for $700 million amid a federal investigation into his wealth management firm. A sale at that price point could set a new record for a WNBA franchise, according to Yahoo Sports. Meanwhile, the Sparks recently secured the sixth and seventh picks in the WNBA draft lottery, according to Basketball News.
