Donald Trump Comments on Potential Tensions Between Argentina and the UK Over Falklands

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Donald Trump raised concerns over potential renewed conflict between Argentina and the United Kingdom regarding the sovereignty of the Falkland Islands (Malvinas), stating following a press briefing in Dublin that he would be willing to intervene if requested by the nations involved.

Speaking to reporters after a meeting with the Irish Prime Minister, Trump questioned whether the United Kingdom would currently be willing to engage in a long-distance conflict as it did in 1982, citing domestic challenges including energy and immigration. "I am sure they will call me to intervene in that potential disaster, but if they do, I can probably resolve it," Trump stated, adding that "right now we have two countries that are not happy." The comments come amid renewed rhetoric surrounding sovereignty issues under Argentine President Javier Milei, as reported by Diario Registrado.

The sovereignty of the Falkland Islands, referred to as Las Malvinas in Argentina, has remained a subject of historical dispute between Buenos Aires and London since the 1982 conflict. British defense representatives previously affirmed that their forces maintain ongoing readiness in the region.
