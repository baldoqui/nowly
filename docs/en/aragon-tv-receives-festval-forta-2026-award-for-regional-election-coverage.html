Aragón TV Receives FesTVal-FORTA 2026 Award for Regional Election Coverage

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Aragón TV received the FesTVal-FORTA 2026 award on Saturday during the closing gala of the Vitoria-Gasteiz Television Festival for its comprehensive news coverage of the regional elections held on February 8.

The award recognizes the largest technical and human operation in the regional network's 20-year history, produced in collaboration with Chip Audiovisual of the HENNEO Group. The election deployment mobilized nearly 200 professionals across three simultaneous sets and more than 20 live locations, incorporating remote production technology, augmented reality, and outdoor drone footage. According to Aragón TV, the election special led its time slot with an average audience share of 30.1 percent over four hours of broadcast, propelling the channel to a 20.1 percent daily share, the highest of any television channel in Spain that day. Executive leadership and news anchors highlighted the recognition as a testament to public service journalism and viewer trust.

The election night broadcast concluded a broader campaign effort by Aragón TV that featured 12 hours of specialized news programming, including head-to-head debates and candidate interviews. The FesTVal-FORTA 2026 awards also honored Murcia's Radiotelevisión de la Región de Murcia for its afternoon entertainment show 'Ailoviu' and Valencia's À Punt for its culinary program 'La Cuina de Morera'.
