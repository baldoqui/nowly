At Least 16 Killed in Chile Nursing Home Fire

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

At least 16 people died and 10 others were evacuated after a fire swept through the El Edén nursing home in Pitrufquén, located in Chile's La Araucanía region.

Seven firefighter units alongside teams from the Emergency Medical Care Service (SAMU) responded to the scene to conduct evacuations and render medical care to affected residents. Authorities have launched an investigation to establish the cause of the blaze and determine what triggered the fire, according to Agencia Noticias Argentinas.

The tragedy follows another severe fire on Thursday at a school in Mogo, in the Kadutu commune, which left 27 people dead.
