Real Madrid Defeats Rayo Vallecano 4-1 Behind Mbappé Double

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Real Madrid defeated Rayo Vallecano 4-1 at the Santiago Bernabéu on matchday five of LaLiga, moving level with Barcelona at the top of the standings with 12 points following a two-goal performance from Kylian Mbappé.

The home side opened the scoring in the 14th minute when Mbappé converted a penalty after Florian Lejeune fouled Álvaro Carreras inside the penalty box. Three minutes later, Carreras doubled the lead by finishing a play after a wall pass with Mbappé. Jude Bellingham added a third goal in the 35th minute, converting a cross from Vinícius Júnior to give Real Madrid a 3-0 lead at halftime. Rayo Vallecano cut the deficit in the 51st minute through Sergio Camello following a defensive giveaway by the hosts. After sustained pressure from the visitors, substitute Arda Güler set up Mbappé in stoppage time to seal the victory with a strike from a tight angle, according to AFP.

The result brings Real Madrid to 12 points in the LaLiga standings, bouncing back from a defeat against Real Betis in the previous fixture. Mbappé's double brought his tally to six goals for the league campaign, placing him tied at the top of the goalscoring table with Rayo Vallecano's Camello and Barcelona's Raphinha.
