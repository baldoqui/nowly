Guardia Civil Arrests Smuggler Transporting 91 Heroin Capsules at Valencia Airport

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

Spanish Guardia Civil officers at Manises Airport in Valencia have arrested a Nigerian national residing in Spain for attempting to smuggle 1,240 grams of heroin inside his body.

The suspect was intercepted during routine drug control checks at the airport terminal after law enforcement officers detected suspicious bulges in his stomach. Following the detection, the detainee was transferred to the General Hospital of Valencia, where medical staff successfully extracted 91 swallowed capsules containing the illicit narcotics, according to Ignacio Cabanes.

The operation is part of ongoing security measures enforced by the Guardia Civil at Valencia's airport to prevent international drug trafficking and dismantle narcotic smuggling routes involving human mules.
