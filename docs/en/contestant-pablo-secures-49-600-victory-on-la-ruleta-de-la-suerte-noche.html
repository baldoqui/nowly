Contestant Pablo Secures €49,600 Victory on La Ruleta de la Suerte Noche

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Contestant Pablo achieved a dramatic comeback victory on the Spanish television game show La ruleta de la suerte noche, accumulating a total grand prize of 49,600 euros.

During the broadcast, fellow contestant Chari initially established a strong lead by building a balance of over 23,000 euros, boosted by a successful performance in the Triple Speed Test and the Question Panel. Despite starting a crucial late round with zero euros, Pablo successfully stole a 100,000-euro wedge from contestant Victor and landed on the jackpot space to add 22,000 euros to his score, securing 35,600 euros to advance to the final round as reported by Antena 3. Pablo then solved the Final Panel to earn an extra 14,000 euros, bringing his total prize money to 49,600 euros.

The special night episode opened with a musical performance by house band member Joaquin Padilla singing his song Te perdi, celebrating his longtime career on the show alongside the host and participants.
