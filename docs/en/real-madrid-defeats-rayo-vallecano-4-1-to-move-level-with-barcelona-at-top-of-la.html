Real Madrid Defeats Rayo Vallecano 4-1 to Move Level with Barcelona at Top of La Liga

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Real Madrid secured a 4-1 victory over Rayo Vallecano at the Santiago Bernabéu on Saturday during the fifth matchday of La Liga, bringing the club level on 12 points with Barcelona at the top of the league standings.

Real Madrid built a commanding 3-0 first-half lead through a penalty from Kylian Mbappé, followed by goals from Álvaro Carreras and Jude Bellingham. Rayo Vallecano cut the deficit early in the second half when Sergio Camello scored following an error in possession by Vinicius, but Mbappé added his second goal near the end of the match to secure the 4-1 win. The victory brought José Mourinho's team to 12 points, temporarily sharing the top spot with Hansi Flick's Barcelona. According to La Liga Match Report, other Saturday action saw Racing Santander defeat Deportivo Alavés 2-1 through a double from Zabiri, Espanyol earn a 2-0 away victory against Osasuna with two goals from Roberto Fernández, and Athletic Bilbao draw 1-1 with Elche following goals by Williams and Facundo Buonanotte.

The fifth round of La Liga fixtures began on Friday with Sevilla defeating Valencia 1-0. Matchday action continues Sunday with Celta Vigo facing Málaga, Barcelona visiting Levante, Getafe hosting Deportivo La Coruña, and Real Sociedad playing Atlético Madrid, before concluding on Monday with Villarreal hosting Real Betis.
