Argentine Police Dismantle Drug Ring That Used Experimental Hydroplane

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Authorities in Argentina have dismantled a drug trafficking network operating between San Nicolás and Rosario following 14 simultaneous raids that resulted in the arrest of six suspects, including the alleged leader, a 29-year-old aerobatic pilot.

According to El Liberal, security forces seized an experimental amphibious Lasermaster Piltri 1 hydroplane valued at $70,000, which was hidden in a private hangar in Alvear and utilized for storing and transporting narcotics. During the raids, police confiscated over 2.1 kilograms of cocaine, 1.8 kilograms of marijuana, 4,050,000 Argentine pesos in cash, three firearms with defaced serial numbers, and a Mercedes-Benz C250 equipped with police sirens and flashing lights to evade traffic checkpoints.

The investigation was conducted by the Buenos Aires Provincial Police in coordination with local judicial authorities, identifying the network's alleged leader as Maximiliano Nicolás Cafiero. Judicial authorities have maintained active international arrest warrants for two additional fleeing suspects connected to the logistics network.
