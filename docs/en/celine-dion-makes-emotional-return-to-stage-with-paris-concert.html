Céline Dion Makes Emotional Return to Stage with Paris Concert

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Canadian singer Céline Dion delivered an emotional performance at the Plenitude Arena in Paris on Saturday, September 12, 2026, marking her first full concert in six years following a prolonged health battle.

The 58-year-old artist opened the concert in front of approximately 35,000 fans with her 1998 track 'On ne change pas' after a 45-minute delay. 'I promised not to cry, but I don't keep my promises. These are tears of joy,' Dion told the audience, according to Reuters. Dion's setlist featured prominent hits including 'Destin' and 'My Heart Will Go On' during the launch of her residency, which encompasses 26 scheduled performances in the French capital, with 16 shows running through October 2026 and an additional ten added for May 2027 due to overwhelming ticket demand.

Dion revealed in 2022 that she was diagnosed with Stiff-Person Syndrome, a rare neurological disorder affecting the central nervous system that causes severe muscle spasms and impacts vocal cords and mobility. Following intensive rehabilitation involving physical and vocal therapy, Dion previously performed Edith Piaf's 'Hymne à l'amour' at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games opening ceremony, but the Plenitude Arena series represents her official full-scale concert residency comeback.
