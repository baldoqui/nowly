Dominican President Inaugurates Los Hierros Bridge to Boost Peravia Infrastructure

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

BANÍ, Dominican Republic — Dominican President Luis Abinader inaugurated the newly constructed Los Hierros bridge in Baní, Peravia province, on Saturday, ending a four-decade wait for local communities and expanding critical transport access across the country's southern region.

Built by engineering firm Ingeniería Fabio García Molina y Asociados with an investment of RD$253 million, the 87.73-meter bridge provides a key alternative route into Baní while improving safety and mobility for northern provincial communities such as Fundación, Río Arriba, El Recodo, and La Gina. Public Works Minister Eduardo Estrella reported that the structure includes anti-erosion protections, sidewalks, drainage, paving, and road signage to ensure long-term durability and prevent the Baní River from posing seasonal flood risks to nearby populations. President Abinader noted that relying on a single bridge for decades created a severe national security bottleneck for the entire southern region, emphasizing that his administration has now added four new river crossings in the area, according to Presidencia RD. Alongside the bridge opening, the president inaugurated the RD$50.5 million Nelson Mateo baseball stadium in Pizarrete and announced strategic progress on the La Gina dam project aimed at securing drinking water supplies for local residents and agricultural producers during dry periods.

Peravia province has historically suffered from low public investment despite its strong agricultural economy and high volume of trade with national markets. The infrastructure push forms part of a broader provincial development initiative that includes the Baní bypass, street paving, hospital renovations, and RD$130 million in additional sports facilities currently under construction.
