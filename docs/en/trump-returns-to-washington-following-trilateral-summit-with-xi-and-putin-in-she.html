Trump Returns to Washington Following Trilateral Summit with Xi and Putin in Shenzhen

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 21:00 UTC · 1 source

U.S. President Donald Trump is returning to Washington, D.C., after holding a trilateral summit in Shenzhen, China, with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum.

According to media reports citing The Guardian and Nikkei Asia, the meeting focused on key global topics including trade, strategic stability, and denuclearization. Discussions centered on reducing nuclear risks and preventing proliferation, with Xi describing the gathering as groundbreaking and emphasizing cooperation among major powers to address global challenges.

Following the summit, Trump described his trip to China as great and highly consequential in a post on Truth Social. He announced plans to hold a press conference at the White House on Friday to discuss incoming trade agreements with Thailand, the Philippines, and other nations.

The gathering was accompanied by media restrictions, as Chinese authorities barred a Bloomberg TV reporter from covering the summit after she asked Trump an unscripted question, declining a White House request to reinstate her access. Reporters traveling with the U.S. delegation also had their mobile phones temporarily confiscated for security reasons as Air Force One departed China.

The trilateral meeting comes amid heightened global focus on trade negotiations and strategic nuclear stability among the United States, China, and Russia.
