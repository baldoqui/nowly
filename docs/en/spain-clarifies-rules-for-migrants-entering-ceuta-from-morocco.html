Spain Clarifies Rules for Migrants Entering Ceuta From Morocco

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Spanish Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska stated that migrants entering the enclave of Ceuta illegally from Morocco will not be allowed to remain or relocate to mainland Spain, subject to strict legal exceptions.

Following the illegal entry of more than 70,000 people from Morocco, Grande-Marlaska affirmed on August 13 that no individuals from the group would remain in Ceuta, be transferred to the mainland, or undergo regularization, according to Undefined Source. However, the minister subsequently qualified his statement by noting that authorities would account for very exceptional cases involving maximum vulnerability, as stipulated under national and international legal frameworks.

Ceuta, a Spanish autonomous city located on the North African coast, represents one of the European Union's two land borders with Africa, making it a frequent focus of regional migration management.
