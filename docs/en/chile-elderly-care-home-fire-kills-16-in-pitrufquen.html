Chile Elderly Care Home Fire Kills 16 in Pitrufquén

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

A catastrophic fire at the El Edén long-term care facility for senior citizens in Pitrufquén, Chile, left 16 residents dead and 10 others hospitalized on Friday night.

The fire broke out around 10:30 p.m. in the Villa Comuy sector of Pitrufquén, located in the southern region of La Araucanía. The residence housed 26 elderly individuals, many of whom suffered severe mobility limitations that hindered their evacuation. Prosecutor Jorge Granada confirmed that ten survivors were rescued with the aid of a center employee before being transferred to a local hospital. Mayor Jacqueline Romero decreed three days of municipal mourning and arranged for Liceo La Frontera to serve as a shelter for affected families, while revealing that the staff member on duty lacked professional nursing credentials. Chilean President José Antonio Kast announced a full investigation into the tragedy and ordered urgent nationwide audits of all elder care facilities, according to Reuters.

The El Edén facility was operating under scrutiny, with regional health officials noting a history of sanitary violations, overcrowding complaints, and administrative penalties dating back to 2019. Municipal authorities had requested a formal inspection in February due to non-compliance with safety codes. Forensic teams from the Legal Medical Service are currently utilizing blood and bone samples to identify the victims while police specialists work to determine the origin of the blaze.
