UD Almería Earns 2-0 Away Win Against Córdoba CF

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

UD Almería secured their first away victory of the LaLiga Hypermotion season on Saturday, defeating Córdoba CF 2-0 at El Arcángel after second-half goals from Brian Cipenga and Thalys.

Córdoba dominated possession during the first half, creating pressure down the right flank through Cristian Carracedo, but failed to capitalize before the interval. The match turned in the 51st minute when Leo Baptistao set up Cipenga to open the scoring for Almería. Córdoba pushed for an equalizer, but goalkeeper Andrés Fernández denied a sharp attempt by Diego Bri. Almería extended their lead in the 70th minute when Javi Muñoz intercepted a ball from Youssouf Diarra and fed substitute Thalys, who finished past goalkeeper Guilherme Fernandes, according to a Match Report. Almería finished the match with ten players after midfielder Quim Junyent received a second yellow card in stoppage time.

The defeat marks Córdoba's third consecutive loss in five matchdays, leaving them near the bottom of the standings with three points. The victory allowed Almería to break their negative away trend and climb the league table.
