Three-Year-Old Girl Injured in Armed Attack in Acámbaro, Guanajuato

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

A three-year-old girl sustained a gunshot wound to her leg on Friday night after armed assailants opened fire on residences in the central district of Acámbaro, Guanajuato.

According to witness reports, gunmen fired bursts of bullets at the facades of at least two homes located on Leandro Valle Street, with one of the projectiles striking the child. Emergency medical personnel and security forces responded to 911 calls, providing initial aid to the victim before transferring her to a local hospital, where her condition remains unspecified by authorities. Law enforcement officers cordoned off the scene as forensics experts from the State Attorney General's Office of Guanajuato collected ballistic evidence to open an investigation and identify the attackers, as reported by Grupo REFORMA.

This shooting follows a separate attack in Acámbaro on September 4, when gunmen targeted a residence on Morales Street in the Chicoasén neighborhood, initially leaving two adults dead and three people gravely injured, including a five-month-old infant. The death toll from that incident later rose to four after two injured victims died in the hospital, leaving the infant as the sole survivor.
