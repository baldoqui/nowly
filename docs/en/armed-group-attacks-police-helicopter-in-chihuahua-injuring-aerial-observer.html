Armed Group Attacks Police Helicopter in Chihuahua, Injuring Aerial Observer

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

An aerial observer for the Chihuahua State Public Security Secretariat was injured on Saturday when armed civilians opened fire on a police helicopter conducting surveillance operations in the Coyame-Ojinaga corridor of Chihuahua, Mexico.

The aircraft sustained multiple gunshot impacts during the attack and was forced to return to the airport in Chihuahua City. The wounded officer was rushed to a medical facility in stable condition to receive treatment for a bullet graze to the head, according to La Parada Digital. State Public Security Secretary Gilberto Loya Chavez condemned the assault as a terrorist act and confirmed that security personnel launched a coordinated land and air operation across the region to track down the assailants.

The surveillance flight was part of ongoing targeted security operations implemented across the Chihuahua, Aldama, Coyame, and Ojinaga corridors following a rise in organized crime activity in recent days. Authorities confirmed that law enforcement patrols and strategic security efforts in the area will continue uninterrupted.
