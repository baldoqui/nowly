Venezuelan Activist Abducted in Caracas After Protesting Near Presidential Palace

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Unidentified armed men forcibly abducted 56-year-old activist Oscar Morales López in central Caracas on Saturday morning, shortly after he participated in a demonstration demanding the release of political prisoners.

Morales López was intercepted near Yanez Bridge in La Candelaria by armed individuals dressed in black who forced him into an unplated white vehicle, according to statements reported by La Patilla. On Friday, Morales López had joined a rally near Miraflores Palace organized by the Committee for the Freedom of Political Prisoners, where he publicly displayed a sign opposing government official Diosdado Cabello. Following his disappearance, his daughter, student leader Itani Morales López, and political figure Juan Pablo Guanipa publicly held Venezuelan authorities responsible for his well-being and demanded immediate proof of life.

The protest involved relatives and activists marching toward the presidential palace amid heavy deployments of the Bolivarian National Guard and National Bolivarian Police, who restricted access as demonstrators sought answers regarding political detentions.
