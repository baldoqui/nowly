Waukesha Holds Final Annual Remembrance Ceremony for 2021 Christmas Parade Victims

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 05:00 UTC · 5 sources

Waukesha, Wisconsin, held its final annual remembrance ceremony on Saturday at Grede Park to honor the six people killed and more than 60 others injured in the 2021 Christmas parade attack. The gathering commenced at 4:39 p.m., marking the exact time emergency responders were called five years ago.

Wisconsin Governor Tony Evers addressed attendees during the ceremony, stating that five years ago the community and state experienced the unimaginable, and emphasized that the victims and the resilience of the community would never be forgotten. Mayor Alicia Halvensleben stated that the decision to conclude the annual city-hosted ceremony was made following consultations with victims and their families. Halvensleben explained that while the city wants to honor the past and respect how the community heals over time, future gatherings may still take place around milestone anniversaries at the memorial.

The tragedy occurred on Nov. 21, 2021, when Darrell E. Brooks Jr. drove through a police barricade and into the parade crowd. The six individuals killed in the attack were Wilhelm Hospel, 81; Virginia Sorenson, 79; Leanna Owen, 71; Tamara Durand, 52; Jane Kulich, 52; and Jackson Sparks, 8.
