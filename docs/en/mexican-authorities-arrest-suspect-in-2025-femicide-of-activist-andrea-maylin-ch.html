Mexican Authorities Arrest Suspect in 2025 Femicide of Activist Andrea Maylin Chino Ramos

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

Mexican federal and state security forces arrested Ricardo 'N' in Puebla on September 12, 2026, for the alleged femicide of 22-year-old activist Andrea Maylin Chino Ramos, ending a 15-month manhunt following the discovery of her body in Morelos in July 2025.

The arrest was announced by Omar García Harfuch, Secretary of Security and Protection Citizenry (SSPC), who detailed that the operation was executed by federal agents alongside the National Intelligence Center (CNI) and the Morelos State Attorney General's Office, in coordination with Puebla authorities and responding to a request from the Secretariat of Women. The suspect was located following crucial information provided by the victim's father, Juan Carlos Chino, after remaining at large since June 2025. According to Quadratín México, Ricardo 'N' was immediately transferred to the state of Morelos to be brought before a judge to face charges for the crime of femicide.

Andrea Maylin Chino Ramos, a mother of a two-year-old girl, disappeared on June 20, 2025, in Yautepec, Morelos, after having previously filed multiple domestic violence complaints and death threat reports against Ricardo 'N'. Her body was recovered on July 31, 2025, buried in a property belonging to the suspect's family in Tlayacapan. Prior to his capture, international alerts had been issued for Ricardo 'N', and both of his parents were arrested and bound over for trial on charges of harboring and aiding a fugitive.
