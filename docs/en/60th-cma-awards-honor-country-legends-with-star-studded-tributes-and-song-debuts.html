60th CMA Awards Honor Country Legends with Star-Studded Tributes and Song Debuts

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 21:00 UTC · 57 sources

Country music stars gathered at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee, on Wednesday, November 18, for the 60th annual CMA Awards, featuring major song debuts, collaborative performances, and tributes to late music icons.

Three-time host Lainey Wilson opened the broadcast alongside Vince Gill with a performance of Dolly Parton's I Will Always Love You to honor the late legend. Wilson also debuted her new single Just A Woman, which was released shortly before the ceremony. In another tribute segment, Zach Top and Dierks Bentley performed Swingin' to honor Country Music Hall of Fame member John Anderson, who died last month at age 71 in Smithville, Tennessee. Top also gave the debut live performance of his new song Moonshine. Additionally, Tim McGraw performed Don't Take The Girl in its entirety on the CMA stage for the first time, coinciding with the surprise release of Don't Take The Girl (Revisited). McGraw also won Musical Event of the Year for McArthur, an all-star collaboration with HARDY, Eric Church, and Morgan Wallen according to event organizers.

The 60th annual CMA Awards aired live on ABC and streamed on Hulu. The event included additional performances by Taylor Swift, Luke Combs, Miranda Lambert, Chris Stapleton, and Kacey Musgraves.
