Bruno Mars Mourns Death of Father Peter Hernandez at Age 74

Saturday, November 21, 2026, 12:00 UTC · 5 sources

Grammy-winning singer Bruno Mars announced the death of his father, Peter Hernandez, who passed away at the age of 74, in a social media post published on November 19, 2026.

In an Instagram tribute, Mars honored his father's role in his life and musical development, sharing that he had hugged him for the final time one week prior to the post. Mars expressed gratitude for the musical knowledge his father imparted and recognized his dedication to raising his six children. The social media tribute included family photographs depicting their relationship throughout the years. The cause of death has not been publicly disclosed.

Hernandez was a musician who introduced Mars to live performance during his early childhood in Hawaii. Mars has consistently credited his family's rich musical upbringing with providing the foundational skills and influences that launched his global career.
