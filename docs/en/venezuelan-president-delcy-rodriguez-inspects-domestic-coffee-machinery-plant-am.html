Venezuelan President Delcy Rodríguez Inspects Domestic Coffee Machinery Plant Amid Export Surge

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

CARACAS — Venezuelan President Delcy Rodríguez conducted an inspection tour of the Deonicio Roaster C.A. manufacturing plant in Miranda state on Saturday to highlight domestic industrial innovation following a reported 186 percent increase in coffee exports in 2025.

During the tour of the 400-square-meter facility located in the Caucagüita parish, Rodríguez stated that domestic coffee production expanded by nearly 10 percent this year, building on the strong export performance of 2025. According to Prensa Presidencial, the manufacturing plant was founded in 2018 by Luis Cortés and Lady Sánchez, employing 11 workers with a production capacity of 15 automated roasting machines per year using computer-aided design, CNC plasma cutting, and specialized welding technology. Rodríguez noted that local production of roasting machinery eliminates the need to import such equipment from European manufacturers in Germany or Italy.

The government inspection forms part of broader economic policies aimed at promoting industrial sovereignty, strengthening public-private partnerships, and integrating communal initiatives with domestic agricultural processing capacity.
