Dominican President Luis Abinader Inaugurates $50.5 Million Baseball Stadium in Peravia

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Dominican President Luis Abinader inaugurated the new Nelson Mateo baseball field in Pizarrete, Peravia province, on Saturday, ending a 26-year wait for local residents and delivering modern sports facilities to benefit approximately 5,000 community members.

The facility was constructed by the Provincial Development Directorate at a cost of RD$50,564,705.40 and includes grandstands, a backstop, two dugouts with restrooms, a cafeteria, a perimeter fence, a 3,000-gallon water cistern, concrete access roads, and night lighting. Speaking at the inauguration, President Abinader encouraged local youth to continue training and highlighted the region's strong record of producing professional baseball players, according to an Official Press Release. José Andújar, director of Provincial Development, stated that active sports infrastructure projects across Peravia currently total RD$130 million, with an additional RD$950 million allocated for 12 upcoming project bidding processes across multiple municipalities.

During the ceremony, Pizarrete Baseball League President Víctor Álvarez presented a recognition plaque to the president, while local officials highlighted broader provincial investments exceeding RD$20 billion. Prior to the event, Abinader also placed a congratulatory phone call to Dominican sprinter Marileidy Paulino following her victory at the World Athletics Ultimate Championship in Budapest, Hungary.
