Ciudad Juárez Urban Restoration Program Reaches 70 Neighborhoods Milestone

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:08 UTC

CIUDAD JUÁREZ, Mexico — The municipal government of Ciudad Juárez extended its public space restoration drive to its 70th neighborhood in Urbivilla del Cedro on Saturday, advancing an urban cleanup program designed to rehabilitate long-neglected infrastructure across the city.

Led by Municipal President Héctor Rafael Ortiz Orpinel, the ongoing 'Juárez Amanece Limpio' initiative mobilizes municipal administrative personnel, public service workers, and federal forces to clean public parks, clear retention dikes, and collect accumulated waste. According to a Gobierno Municipal report, Public Services Director César Alberto Tapia Martínez confirmed that prior phases of the municipal campaign successfully removed over 17,000 tons of rubble, sediment, and trash, along with more than 30,000 discarded tires. Recent operations extended across several key sectors, including Senderos de San Isidro and Lomas del Desierto, where workers cleared drainage dikes and constructed sports facilities to prevent illegal refuse dumping.

The municipal drive maintains a goal of intervening in 110 neighborhoods by December to address decades of infrastructure deficit in the city's southeastern zone. Local authorities emphasized that public maintenance of cleaned retention dikes is critical to preventing urban flooding and roadway damage during rainstorms.
