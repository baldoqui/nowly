Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Warns AI Risk Could Control the Internet

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Anthropic Chief Executive Dario Amodei has called for a deceleration in the pace of artificial intelligence development, warning that unmonitored systems could potentially take control of the entire internet.

Writing in an article published on his website, Amodei warned that without adequate oversight, AI advancement could quickly surpass human ability to comprehend and govern these complex systems. His statement was swiftly supported by other key industry leaders, including Elon Musk and Sam Altman, as reported by T. Nieva.

The warning highlights growing anxiety among leading tech executives regarding the accelerated development of frontier AI models and the critical necessity for robust safety guardrails.
