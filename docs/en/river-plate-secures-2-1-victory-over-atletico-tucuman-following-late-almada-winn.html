River Plate Secures 2-1 Victory Over Atlético Tucumán Following Late Almada Winner

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

River Plate claimed a dramatic 2-1 victory against Atlético Tucumán on Saturday at the Monumental José Fierro stadium in the ninth matchday of the tournament, secured by a 90th-minute goal from Thiago Almada.

The match shifted early in the 12th minute when referee Andrés Gariano issued a direct red card to Atlético Tucumán forward Franco Nicola following a dangerous high-boot challenge against River Plate defender Lucas Martínez Quarta. River Plate capitalized on the numerical advantage in the 21st minute when 19-year-old Tobías Andrada finished a team combination following a cross from Gonzalo Montiel to score the opening goal. Atlético Tucumán managed to equalize in the 83rd minute through a header from Clever Ferreira off a Lautaro Godoy set piece. However, in the 90th minute, Thiago Almada converted an opportunity inside the penalty area to restore River Plate's lead and seal the win. As reported by TNT Sports, the match also saw disciplinary controversy during the second half when referee Gariano issued only a yellow card to River midfielder Mauro Arambarri for a tackle on Alexis Canelo, leading to the ejection of Atlético Tucumán head coach Julio César Falcioni for his protests.

The win represents River Plate's third consecutive victory under interim manager Leonardo Ponzio, who assumed control following the departure of Eduardo Coudet. The victory elevates River Plate to 13 points and fifth place in Group B, positioning the club in playoff contention while ending a seven-year winless streak in Tucumán.
