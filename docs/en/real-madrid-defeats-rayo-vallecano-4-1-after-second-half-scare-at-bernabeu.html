Real Madrid Defeats Rayo Vallecano 4-1 After Second-Half Scare at Bernabeu

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

MADRID — Real Madrid secured a 4-1 victory over Rayo Vallecano at the Santiago Bernabeu on Saturday in LaLiga, led by a brace from Kylian Mbappe alongside goals from Alvaro Carreras and Jude Bellingham, despite experiencing a second-half slump that drew whistles from the home crowd.

The hosts took control early in the first half following back-to-back defensive errors by Rayo Vallecano defender Florian Lejeune. Lejeune conceded a penalty in the 13th minute after bringing down Carreras inside the box, allowing Mbappe to open the scoring from the spot. Three minutes later, another miscue by Lejeune led to Carreras doubling Real Madrid's lead following a quick interchange with Mbappe. Bellingham extended the advantage to 3-0 in the 35th minute after finishing a low cross from Vinicius Jr. However, Real Madrid's intensity dropped after the break, enabling Rayo Vallecano's Sergio Camello to pull a goal back in the 50th minute after capitalizing on a turnover. Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho introduced Arda Guler in the second half to restore control, and Mbappe sealed the victory deep into stoppage time by scoring on a counterattack, as reported by EUROPA PRESS.

The fixture marked the first starting appearance of the season for Real Madrid winger Yan Diomande, who featured in an attacking group alongside Mbappe, Bellingham, and Vinicius Jr. The win returns Real Madrid to victory in domestic competition following a recent league loss to Real Betis and a mid-week UEFA Champions League match against Inter Milan.
