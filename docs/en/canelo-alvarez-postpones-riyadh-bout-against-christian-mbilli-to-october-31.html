Canelo Alvarez Postpones Riyadh Bout Against Christian Mbilli to October 31

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Mexican boxer Saul 'Canelo' Alvarez has postponed his upcoming super middleweight fight against Christian Mbilli from September 12 to October 31 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, citing personal family issues that prevented him from entering training camp on time.

Speaking in an interview with Uncrowned, Alvarez explained that he needed to resolve family matters before committing to his training routine, forcing the date change. The 36-year-old Mexican fighter stated that his personal situation is now settled and that he has actively resumed training for the rescheduled October 31 event, which will be broadcast globally on DAZN pay-per-view.

Alvarez last fought in September in Las Vegas, where he lost his super middleweight titles to Terence Crawford. His upcoming opponent, the 31-year-old Cameroonian-Canadian Mbilli, enters the ring undefeated in 30 professional bouts with a record of 29 wins, zero losses, and one draw.
