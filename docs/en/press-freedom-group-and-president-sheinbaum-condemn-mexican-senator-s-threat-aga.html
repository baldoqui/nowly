Press Freedom Group and President Sheinbaum Condemn Mexican Senator's Threat Against Journalist

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Press freedom organization Articulo 19 and Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum have publicly condemned Morena Party Senator Luis Fernando Salazar following online threats he directed at journalist Enrique Acevedo over an investigative news report.

The controversy began on September 8 when Senator Salazar published a message on X targeting Acevedo, stating that "we are going to go after you for being a vulgar liar" after the journalist presented a report on the news program N+ Focus regarding Senator Julieta Ramirez. Salazar deleted the post and issued a public apology on September 10, claiming his comment referred solely to legal avenues. According to El Universal, Articulo 19 urged the lawmaker not to abuse his public role and demanded that public officials undergo human rights training to ensure a safe environment for reporters. During a press conference in Chiapas on September 11, President Sheinbaum reiterated that public servants must never use threatening language against journalists, regardless of any editorial disagreements.

The investigative report presented by Acevedo focused on Senator Ramirez, a candidate for the governorship of Baja California, alleging a potential agreement between her and U.S. authorities. Articulo 19 highlighted that under International Freedom of Expression principles, threats against journalists represent a direct attack on freedom of speech, underscoring the Mexican state's duty to prevent, investigate, and sanction such acts.
