Trump Warns Falkland Islands Dispute Could Be 'Potential Disaster,' Offers to Mediate

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

U.S. President Donald Trump warned on Saturday in Dublin that any escalation of the sovereignty dispute between Argentina and the United Kingdom over the Falkland Islands would be a potential disaster, offering to intervene as a mediator if requested.

Speaking during an official visit alongside Irish Prime Minister Micheál Martin, Trump expressed confidence in his ability to resolve tensions if called upon by both nations. He pointed to the geographic distance involved in deploying forces to the South Atlantic and suggested that domestic issues in Britain, such as energy and immigration, might deter military action. According to AFP, Trump had previously indicated in late August that the United States was reviewing its historical position of neutrality regarding sovereignty over the archipelago.

The Falkland Islands, known in Argentina as the Islas Malvinas, were the site of a 74-day war in 1982 that resulted in a British victory and cost the lives of 649 Argentine and 255 British personnel. Recent diplomatic friction has escalated following Argentine legal actions against offshore oil exploration companies operating under British licenses and proposed naval developments in southern Argentina.
