Morena Leader Criticizes Opposition PAN President Over European Tour

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

MEXICO CITY — Morena national leader Ariadna Montiel publicly criticized National Action Party (PAN) President Jorge Romero on September 12, 2026, for traveling across Europe to build foreign political alliances at the start of Mexico's 2026-2027 electoral process.

Montiel argued that the opposition leadership chose to travel thousands of kilometers away from Mexico rather than connecting directly with citizens on the ground. In a statement detailed by a News Report, Montiel asserted that Mexican democracy is decided domestically through the free and sovereign vote of the people, accusing PAN of lacking a cohesive national project. Romero defended his European tour, which included a meeting in Germany with Konrad Adenauer Foundation officials Mark Speich and Caroline Kanter, as an effort to strengthen his party and raise concerns regarding the current Mexican administration.

The trip follows an earlier visit on September 8, when Romero and other opposition figures, including former Mexico City mayoral candidate Santiago Taboada, presented allegations to the European Parliament in Brussels regarding political conditions in Mexico under Morena.
