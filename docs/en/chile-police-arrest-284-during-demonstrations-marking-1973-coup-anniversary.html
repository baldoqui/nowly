Chile Police Arrest 284 During Demonstrations Marking 1973 Coup Anniversary

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Chilean authorities arrested 284 people during demonstrations on Friday, September 11, commemorating the 53rd anniversary of the 1973 military coup, representing an 80 percent increase in detentions compared to 2025.

Chilean Security Minister Martín Arrau toured major protest sites in Santiago alongside police commanders on Saturday following demonstrations in areas including Villa Francia, Cerro Navia, and Valparaíso. Arrau stated on social media that nationwide violent events fell by 34 percent compared to the previous year, despite the surge in arrests. The 284 detainees included 16 foreign nationals and 31 minors, with authorities reporting that some individuals possessed Molotov cocktails and pyrotechnics. While 198 violent incidents were recorded, officials confirmed that no civilians were injured and no public transportation infrastructure was damaged, according to Europa Press.

The commemorations marked the first anniversary of the coup since far-right President José Antonio Kast took office following the four-year term of leftist President Gabriel Boric. Historical records indicate that more than 3,200 people were killed in the state repression that followed the September 11, 1973 military coup led by Armed Forces chief Augusto Pinochet against democratically elected socialist President Salvador Allende.
