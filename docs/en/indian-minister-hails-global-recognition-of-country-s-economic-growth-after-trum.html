Indian Minister Hails Global Recognition of Country's Economic Growth After Trump Comments

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 19:30 UTC · 5 sources

Indian Union Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat said on Sunday that India's economic momentum is gaining global recognition, responding to recent comments by U.S. President Donald Trump referencing India's strong economic performance.

Speaking to reporters in Jaipur, Shekhawat highlighted recent growth forecasts from major international financial bodies. According to statements cited from the press briefing, the World Bank has projected India's Gross Domestic Product growth at over 7.1 percent, the International Monetary Fund estimates it at 7.2 percent, and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development forecasts growth exceeding 7 percent.

Shekhawat emphasized that the Indian economy continues to advance despite international headwinds, including geopolitical instability, supply chain disruptions, and global energy security challenges. He expressed confidence that India's actual performance by the end of the year would exceed estimates made by international institutions.

The minister's remarks followed comments made by President Trump at the White House, where the U.S. leader cited India's low interest rates and 7 percent GDP growth while criticizing the Federal Reserve's monetary policy. Trump argued that high borrowing costs restrict U.S. economic potential, alleging the central bank acts politically by raising interest rates whenever positive economic figures are announced.

Trump's criticism of the Federal Reserve comes as U.S. inflation remains above the central bank's target. Data cited in his speech showed the annual Personal Consumption Expenditures inflation rate at 3.6 percent in September, with core PCE inflation standing at 3.1 percent.
