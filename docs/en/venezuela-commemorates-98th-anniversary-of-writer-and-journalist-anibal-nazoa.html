Venezuela Commemorates 98th Anniversary of Writer and Journalist Aníbal Nazoa

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Venezuela honored the memory and legacy of prominent writer, journalist, and humorist Aníbal Nazoa on Sept. 12, marking the 98th anniversary of his birth.

Born in Caracas in 1928, Nazoa devoted his career to depicting daily Venezuelan life, utilizing language as a tool for social analysis and popular commitment. Alongside his brother, poet Aquiles Nazoa, he elevated traditional humor into critical literature through press columns including "Aquí hace calor" and "Puerta de Caracas," which examined political, cultural, and urban affairs during the second half of the 20th century. According to Ciudad CCS, Nazoa also served as a writer and key collaborator for satirical print publications such as "El Morrocoy Azul," "La Pava Macha," and "El Sádico Ilustrado," while participating actively in national radio broadcasting.

Nazoa's literary work remains compiled in publications such as "Obras incompletas," preserving his legacy in Venezuelan culture, intellectual critique, and collective memory.
