Former Valencian President Ximo Puig Marries Former Justice Minister Gabriela Bravo

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

Former President of the Generalitat Valenciana Ximo Puig and former regional Minister of Justice Gabriela Bravo were married on Saturday at a seaside venue in Oliva, Spain, gathered alongside prominent figures from the Spanish Socialist Workers' Party.

The ceremony took place under strict security controls to manage guest access amid beachgoers and onlookers. According to Levante - EMV, attendees included current Treasury Minister Arcadi España, Secretary of State for Communication Lydia del Canto, national deputy Vicent Sarrià, and Mediterranean Corridor Commissioner Joan Calabuig, alongside long-standing party figures such as Antoni Such and former Gandia Mayor José Manuel Orengo. Government delegate Pilar Bernabé was among the notable absences at the event.

Both Puig and Bravo have held prominent positions in Valencian politics. Bravo served as Justice Minister during Puig's Botànic coalition governments before stepping down from active politics to rejoin the fiscal service. Puig left his post as Secretary General of the regional Socialist party and has served as Spain's Ambassador to the OECD in Paris since February 2024.
