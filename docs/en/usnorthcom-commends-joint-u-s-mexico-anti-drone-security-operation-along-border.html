USNORTHCOM Commends Joint U.S.-Mexico Anti-Drone Security Operation Along Border

September 13, 2026 · Spanish sources

The United States Northern Command praised Mexican authorities and interagency partners on Saturday, September 12, 2026, for joint operations aimed at detecting and neutralizing drug cartel drones used for narcotics smuggling and human trafficking along the shared southern border.

In a statement posted on social media platform X, USNORTHCOM endorsed comments made by U.S. Ambassador to Mexico Ronald Johnson regarding Operation High Eagle, a binational initiative countering criminal drone activities between Tijuana, Baja California, and San Diego, California. According to Proceso, Mexican Secretary of National Defense Ricardo Trevilla Trejo detailed that Mexico deployed roughly 800 military personnel armed with fixed and mobile anti-drone equipment capable of inhibiting signal transmissions within a three-kilometer radius. Both nations are executing coordinated monitoring and defensive operations strictly within their respective sovereign territories to tackle evolving cartel technology.

Operation High Eagle launched its first phase between August 31 and September 2 with joint coordination exercises, followed by a second phase active through September 21. The operation focuses on a five-kilometer zone centered around Mesa de Otay to suppress cartel surveillance and illicit border crossings.
