Thousands Gather in Oviedo to Protest Assault on Former Councilor and Activist

Sunday, September 13, 2026, 00:07 UTC

Thousands of people demonstrated in Oviedo's Cathedral Square to protest a violent assault against former Somos Oviedo city councilor Rubén Rosón and activist Jorge Fernández, who were attacked two days earlier after confronting a group tearing down a rainbow flag and an anti-racist poster.

According to organizers, approximately 5,000 people joined the demonstration initially called under the slogan 'Asturies sin miedo' by the collective Fiestes Populares Uviéu. The protest later evolved into a march through several busy city streets during local festival celebrations. The gathering brought together members of various political parties, including Festejos Councilor Covadonga Díaz, Security Councilor José Ramón Prado, former politician Gaspar Llamazares, PSOE mayoral candidate Sergio Llera, and Izquierda Unida federal coordinator Antonio Maíllo. During his address to the crowd, Rosón condemned the violence and urged the city to reject hatred, as reported by Local News. Rosón also called on Oviedo Mayor Alfredo Canteli to reinstate the rainbow-painted benches at La Escandalera as a symbol of inclusion before the conclusion of the San Mateo festivities.

The violent encounter occurred when Rosón and Fernández reproached a group of approximately eight youths who were removing symbols from their collective's booth in the square. The incident has sparked broader debates among local representatives regarding political violence and intolerance in the region.
