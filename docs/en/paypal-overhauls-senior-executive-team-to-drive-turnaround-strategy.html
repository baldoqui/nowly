PayPal Overhauls Senior Executive Team to Drive Turnaround Strategy

Sunday, November 22, 2026, 05:00 UTC · 5 sources

SAN JOSE, California — PayPal has restructured its executive leadership team by naming Xero executive Claire Bramley as chief financial officer alongside new appointments from American Express and Brightstar.AI, the payments company announced on November 20.

Bramley, who has served as CFO at global small-business platform Xero since April 2025, will join PayPal on February 1, succeeding Jamie Miller. In her new role, Bramley will lead PayPal's multi-year initiative to streamline operations, reduce expenses, and improve margins, aiming to deliver at least 1.5 billion USD in gross run-rate savings over the next two to three years. PayPal also named American Express executive Kartik Mani as president and head of consumer financial services and Venmo. Additionally, Juan Rio will join from Brightstar.AI at the end of November as executive vice-president of AI business transformation and operations. Integrating artificial intelligence and automation across PayPal's business accounts is projected to account for about 40 percent of the overall savings target.

The executive reshuffle comes as PayPal works to regain market share lost to smartphone-embedded digital payment platforms, including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Analysts at TD Cowen noted that the leadership transitions introduce near-term organizational disruption, positioning 2027 as another transition year for the company.
